# Evidence boundaries

- Primary three-seed accuracy and controlled perturbation metrics are quantitative evidence under the stated dataset protocol.
- The 1409-image archive is secondary-domain transfer evidence, not verified full external validation.
- The 23 real-scene photos are unlabeled qualitative operator-assisted classification demonstrations.
- K230 measurements describe the tested seed-43 PTQ artifact and board input path: 60-minute no-observed-error run, 3.14 W estimated system-input power, and 39.1 degC maximum surface temperature in the captured thermal image.
- The PTQ artifact showed calibration-dependent full-test accuracy degradation; no deployment accuracy superiority is claimed.
- Peak memory and CPU/KPU utilization were not available from the tested firmware.
